Privacy Policy — Shady LinkedIn AI

Last updated: October 9, 2026

1. About This Project

Shady LinkedIn AI is a planned personal project intended to help its owner prepare professional LinkedIn posts based on work-related notes supplied by the owner.

2. Planned Functionality

The initial version is intended to help prepare and review draft posts before any post is published. The project may later connect to LinkedIn or an AI service, subject to the relevant platform permissions and technical requirements.

3. Information and Data

The project is being developed. The information collected, processed, transmitted, or stored will depend on the features implemented. This policy will be updated before those features are introduced to accurately describe the data involved and how it is handled.

Users should not submit confidential company information, customer personal data, passwords, or access tokens unless an appropriate and authorized process has been established.

4. Third-Party Services

Future versions may use third-party services, such as LinkedIn or an AI provider. If such services are integrated, this policy will be updated to identify the relevant services and explain the applicable data handling practices.

5. Security and Data Retention

Security measures and data retention practices will be documented based on the actual implementation before personal data is processed or stored by the application.

6. Contact

For privacy questions about this personal project, contact the project owner through the GitHub repository where this policy is published.
